Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers before we even start.

How much does a custom web application cost?

Because every project is unique, we provide fixed-cost quotes after our initial discussion. Small business landing pages typically start around ₦150,000, while complex systems like multi-vendor marketplaces or custom POS software range between ₦500,000 and ₦2M+.

How long does it take to build a system?

A standard website takes 1-2 weeks. A complex web application or marketplace takes 4-8 weeks depending on the features. We provide a clear timeline in your quote and send you weekly review builds so you can see the progress.

Do you offer maintenance after launch?

Yes. We offer monthly maintenance retainers to handle server updates, bug fixes, and minor content changes. We ensure your software remains secure and online long after it is deployed.

Can you customize software you already built?

Absolutely. If you want to license the LegisCore engine for your law firm, or adapt the Asad POS for your retail store, we can customize the branding, workflows, and user roles to fit your specific business.

I don't live in Yola. Can we still work together?

Yes. While our studio is based in Jimeta-Yola, we work remotely with clients in Abuja, Lagos, Kano, and across Nigeria. All communication, reviews, and project handoffs are handled smoothly via WhatsApp and Email.
